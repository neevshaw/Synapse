Lesson 1.1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding natural language, recognizing images and speech, problem-solving, decision-making, and learning from experience.

Example: When you ask Siri or Alexa a question, AI processes your voice, interprets it, and generates a response almost instantly.

AI systems are categorized into two main types: Narrow AI, which specializes in one task (like recommendation systems), and General AI, a theoretical system capable of any human-like intellectual task.

Key components of AI include:

  • Data: Large datasets allow AI to identify patterns.
  • Algorithms: Mathematical instructions that guide learning and decision-making.
  • Computation: Powerful hardware enables processing of complex calculations.

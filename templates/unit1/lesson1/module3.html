Lesson 1.3: Real-World Applications of AI

AI is everywhere today, transforming industries in ways we often take for granted. Some sectors use AI to analyze massive datasets, while others rely on it for automation and intelligent decision-making.

Healthcare: AI algorithms can detect anomalies in medical scans faster than human radiologists, helping diagnose diseases earlier and more accurately.

In retail, AI predicts which products will sell and optimizes stock levels. Recommendation engines on e-commerce sites suggest products based on your browsing habits, increasing both convenience and sales.

Fun Fact: Netflix uses AI not just for recommendations, but also to decide which thumbnails to show you for each title!

Transportation and logistics also benefit from AI. Self-driving vehicles combine sensors, cameras, and real-time traffic data to navigate safely. AI also optimizes delivery routes, reducing fuel consumption and delivery times.

"AI is the new electricity." – Andrew Ng

Finally, AI is moving into creative fields. It can compose music, generate artwork, and even write short stories. This illustrates that AI is not just about efficiency — it’s also about augmenting human creativity.
